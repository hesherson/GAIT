GAIT 1.8.0-alpha2 validation

HEMTT 1.21.0: PASS; config 1.8.0.2, 12 SQF files, one PBO.
Requires Arma 3 2.18 for array switchMove and getUnitMovesInfo.
Build warnings are style suggestions; bundled wiki fallback was used.

Preservation: PASS including mutation checks. All 25 historical reference
hashes retained: 22 unchanged feature blocks and three blocks with four exact
reviewed exceptions for the requested momentum/downhill changes. Two original
tuning files are byte-identical; all prior CBA setting defaults are retained.
Brace duration/dip/snap/kit relief, ACE reserve gates, Shift taper, sway,
hearing, trip/landing behavior and vegetation tuning remain protected.

Graph: PASS; 72 native-derived states and eight action maps, preserving the
original 36 sprint/run/idle states and adding 36 short walk/idle brace states.
Four config-contract groups and generator consistency pass.

Ten SQF-VM actual-function regression suites: PASS
- brace_momentum: physical history, uphill low coefficient, downhill high
  actual speed with low coefficient, all-trigger veto, single zero-speed
  frame, real stop/rearm, settled walking, first press and wall/unsafe context
- downhill_pace: 797 checks including angle/load order, bounded steep descent,
  existing pace floor, time-step consistency, stall cap and moving recovery
- locomotion_handoff: aim/phase arguments, 12 lifecycle cases, one brace and
  promotion, active-family stop/restart, 36 brace selections
- locomotion_state_machine: held W/A/D/idle, entry/release, escape and handoff
- release_and_blend: inherited argument safety and allowed/unsafe blends
- input_and_ramp: immediate directions and time-based coefficient ramp
- slope_direction: 16 selections and reversal/release
- slope_state_selection: 12 sprint and 20 side/rear run states
- locomotion_pace: fallback/physical-unit interface and invalid calibration
- ace_status_bridge: initialization order, medical masks and physiology owner

Recorder syntax is checked separately. Built PBO prefix and all 12 embedded
scripts are compared with source during packaging; archive hashes are checked.

SQF-VM uses a dummy registry entry for getUnitMovesInfo solely to parse the
new command. It does not execute the live animation helper or simulate Arma
physics. Tests do not establish camera smoothness, visible brace duration,
actual terrain speed, animation-mod compatibility or multiplayer behavior.
Windows installer execution and in-game acceptance remain target checks.
