# GAIT

Ground-momentum ACE Integrated Traversal for Arma 3. GAIT adjusts movement for terrain, carried load, and sprint reserve. ACE supplies fatigue feedback.

**Dead sprint.** While moving forward, double-tap the bound sprint key within 0.32 seconds and hold the second press. With a rifle, the standing runner tucks the stock under the right arm and drives the left arm through the stride. The entry leans forward, the downhill stride straightens, and dwindling reserve shortens the free-arm swing. A heavy load brings that tired stride on earlier. Authored weapon and vest profiles adjust stock clearance. Hand signals, ready positions, reloads and other active gestures take priority through the rest of the burst. Regular jogging and single-press sprinting use the normal Arma animations. The dead sprint uses a half-length brace. Aiming, switching weapons, and changing stance can interrupt it.

The free hand stays loosely curled through the stride. As the elbow comes forward, the shoulder turns inward and the forearm follows, tipping the palm slightly down near the chest. The rifle catch opens the support hand. With a pistol the runner draws the firing hand close to the chest. With a launcher both hands support the tube. The exhausted rifle runner lets the free arm hang lower and angles the rifle forward. Chest movement follows the delivered respiratory rate from ACME/ACM when available (visual ceiling 75 breaths/min). ACE Advanced Fatigue supplies a normalized effort estimate on other clients. GAIT uses reserve if neither medical source is available.

A qualifying double-tap drops an ACE carry or drag, including a Fast Carry pickup. It can cancel an ACE cargo deployment, placement preview or progress bar and resume once movement input is live. The item stays in the world when dropped. The object's owning addon handles cleanup.

Downhill speed follows your direction of travel and fades as you turn across a slope. GAIT reads the walkable surface beneath your feet on ramps and bridges. A level deck stays level over sloping terrain. Carrying or dragging a patient adds a gradual effort allowance for their body and kit. Dragging uses a smaller share because the ground supports the patient.

A dead sprint costs one 20% segment over its first second, even if released early. Sustained flat-ground drain ranges from 5% to 8% of full reserve per second with load. GAIT's travel grade reduces effort on descents and increases it on climbs, up to 10% per second. The dead sprint builds over several steps, peaks about 9% above the fresh level-ground sprint for the equipped kit, and settles to a 3.5% advantage while held. The final 20% of reserve blends back to ordinary pace and drain. Earned downhill momentum can briefly reach 30.8 km/h. Downhill trip exposure begins at 14 degrees during a full dead sprint and uses actual speed, gear, and fatigue.

The `dev/gait-foundation-1.8.0` branch provides the ACE-feedback-only build. See [build and deployment](README_HEMTT.md) for the one-time Git setup and the pull-and-deploy commands.
